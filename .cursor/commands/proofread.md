4. **Flow**: Ensure smooth transitions between sentences and ideas
5. **Consistency**: Check for consistent tone, style, and terminology
6. **Word Choice**: Suggest stronger or more precise vocabulary when helpful

Please provide:
- The corrected text
- A brief explanation of major changes
- Any suggestions for overall improvement
