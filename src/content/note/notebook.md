If you're curious how notebooks enabled innovation throughout history, Roland Allen's [The Notebook: A History of Thinking on Paper](https://a.co/d/0iu8HF7m) is a great read! One standout chapter describes how notebooks were central to the prosperity and creativity in Florence, Italy.
