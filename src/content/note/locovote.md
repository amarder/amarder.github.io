When my family was considering where to put down roots in Massachusetts, I spent time digging through MCAS data to better understand the schools in towns we were exploring. I thought others might find this data useful too, so I created Locovote.

The tool makes it easy to compare school districts and town finances (see examples below). Check it out at https://locovote.com/ and let me know what you think!
