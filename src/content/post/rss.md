- Remember Google Reader before it was shut down? That was awesome.
- Remember Twitter before it was sold? That was awesome.
- What's awesome now?

I've been enjoying [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/andrewmarder.net), but it leaves me wanting more. And I don't really want to invest in a platform that's likely to get worse over time. That's why I'm investing in RSS so I can subscribe to feeds and get awesome content delivered to me in a way that lets me focus on reading.

## Software

I'm using [Miniflux](https://miniflux.app/) to aggregate my RSS feeds. I tried [Capy Reader](https://github.com/jocmp/capyreader) and [Read You](https://github.com/ReadYouApp/ReadYou) to get a better UI on my Android phone but was having minor issues with both. Right now the web interface to Miniflux is good enough for me.
