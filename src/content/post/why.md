:::note{title=TLDR}
Tech is political. I want tech that is compatible with a government that prioritizes people over corporations. Also, I enjoy tinkering with computers.
:::

Below is a mental model that helps me think about life in the USA. It contains three entities: People, Corporations, and Government.

- People vote for Government representatives and choose what goods and services to buy from Corporations.
- Corporations lobby the Government and choose which People to employ.
- The Government taxes People and Corporations, decides what public projects to fund, and enacts laws that affect both Corporations and People.

Like Abraham Lincoln, I want "government of the people, by the people, for the people." I believe current policies favor corporations over people. I want that to change. I care about people (I do not care about corporations or the government). I worry that my ability to enact change through voting is somewhat limited. So, this blog focuses on what goods and services I consume. Here are some changes I've made since starting this blog:

| Category             | Before             | After                               |
|----------------------|--------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Web search           | Google             | DuckDuckGo                          |
| Web browser          | Chrome             | [Firefox](/firefox)                 |
| Email                | Gmail              | [Fastmail](/fastmail)               |
| Photos               | Google Photos      | [Immich](/immich)                   |
| Mobile OS            | Android            | [GrapheneOS](/grapheneos)           |
| News                 | The New York Times | The Guardian                        |
| Music                | Spotify            | Qobuz                               |

Living in the attention economy, blogging has helped me reclaim some of my attention so I can focus on what matters to me.
