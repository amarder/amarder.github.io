What is [Meshtastic](https://meshtastic.org/)?

> Meshtastic® is a project that enables you to use inexpensive LoRa radios as a long range off-grid communication platform in areas without existing or reliable communications infrastructure. This project is 100% community driven and open source!

Remember when you were asking how can we communicate privately when the telecommunications grid goes down? Meshtastic is the answer! I'm interested in Meshtastic because part of me wants to be fully prepared in case of emergency. I was also curious to see if other folks near me have set up Meshtastic nodes. In August of 2024, I bought a [Heltec v3](https://heltec.org/project/wifi-lora-32-v3/) for $26 and I took it out on a few bike rides and found no one. The Heltec v3 alone was annoying because I had to plug it into my phone to use it. So, then I decided I needed:

1. [Battery](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DMCXJMGF) for $24
2. 3D-printed case from [PacificNorthWest3D](https://www.etsy.com/shop/PacificNorthWest3D) for $20
3. [Antenna](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B086ZG5WBR) for $12

For both the battery and the antenna the cheapest option I could find was a four pack of each. All in, I ended up spending $82. I now had a Meshtastic radio I could bring anywhere. I brought it on a few more bike rides, some car rides, and left it on in my house. Unfortunately, I still have not communicated directly with anyone on the mesh. I've enjoyed watching [Atlavox](https://atlavox.com/) develop their solar-powered Meshtastic node [Beacon](https://atlavox.com/products/atlavox-beacon-solar-meshtastic-node). But at this point, I'd label myself a failed Meshtastic user. So, dear reader, here are my questions for you:

1. What should I do with my Meshtastic radio?
2. For new users with a budget around $100, what Meshtastic products would you suggest? I would suggest not following in my footsteps.
3. If you're a Meshtastic user near Wayland Massachusetts, please reach out. I'd love to connect!
