I just deployed Uptime Kuma and it's awesome!

I've created a [status page](https://status.andrewmarder.net/status/mardnet) that monitors the uptime of my blog and newsletter. Here are some features I really appreciate:

- **Easy to use** and configure
- Straightforward **status pages** that feel familiar
- History of **ping times** so I can see how responsive my sites are
- **Email alerts** so I know if my sites go down

If you're looking for a self-hosted monitoring tool, [Uptime Kuma](https://github.com/louislam/uptime-kuma) is where it's at!
